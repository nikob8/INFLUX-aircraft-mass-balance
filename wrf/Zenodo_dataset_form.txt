Resource type:  Dataset

Title:  WRF-Chem fields for the INFLUX aircraft mass balance flights, 2011 to 2016

Creators:  the authors of the paper, with affiliations and ORCIDs

Description:
Subset of the WRF-Chem (version 3.6.1) simulations used in Balashov, Barkley,
Davis and Shepson (2026), "Evaluation of aircraft-based methane emissions
estimates and estimation methodologies with six years of data from Indianapolis",
Journal of Geophysical Research: Atmospheres. One MATLAB (HDF5) file per flight
day, 37 files, holds the fields the analyses of the paper use: earth-relative
wind, potential temperature, height, pressure and boundary layer height on the
lowest 25 levels of the 3 km domain within 160 km of Indianapolis (the whole
3 km domain on 11/19/2014), every hour from 8 h before the first aircraft
transect to 1 h after the last one, and the eight CH4 tracers (five regional
source categories of the EPA gridded inventory and three Marion County tracers)
during the flight hours. WRF_configuration.txt documents the complete model
configuration of both domains; WRF_subsets_README.txt describes every variable
and gives reading examples for MATLAB and Python. The analysis code that reads
these files is archived separately (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.[[record number]]).

Keywords:  methane; WRF-Chem; Indianapolis; INFLUX; aircraft mass balance; urban emissions
